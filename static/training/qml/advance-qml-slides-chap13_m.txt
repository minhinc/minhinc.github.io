 <pre " class="slidecontent">Arranging Items
Positioners and repeaters make it easier to work with many items.
 <b>-Item Positioners</b>
 
<pre class="note">Positioner items are container items that manage the positions of items in a declarative user interface. Positioners behave in a similar way to the layout managers used with standard Qt widgets, except that they are also containers(<span style="color:#004000;">Item</span> subclassed) in their own right.</pre>
 
Set of Positioners
<span style="color:#004000;">LayoutMirroring</span> Property used to mirror layout behavior
<span style="color:#004000;">Column</span> Positions its children in a column
<span style="color:#004000;">Flow</span> Positions its children side by side, wrapping as necessary
<span style="color:#004000;">Grid</span> Positions its children in grid formation
<span style="color:#004000;">Positioner</span> Provides attached properties that contain details on where an item exists in a positioner
<span style="color:#004000;">Row</span> Positions its children in a row
 
LayoutMirroring and Positioner are attached property used to provide mirroring and give special information to Positioners Item containers,i.e. Rectangle, respectively.
LayoutMirroring
The <span style="color:#004000;">LayoutMirroring</span> attached property is used to horizontally mirror Item anchors, positioner types (such as Row and Grid) and views (such as GridView and horizontal ListView). Mirroring is a visual change: left anchors become right anchors, and positioner types like Grid and Row reverse the horizontal layout of child items.
